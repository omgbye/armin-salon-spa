Accessibility

Last updated November 12, 2026

Armin Salon & Rejuvenation wants everyone to be able to use this website and enjoy the salon. This page describes what we do to make the site accessible — and how to reach us if anything gets in your way.

Our commitment

We aim for this website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, the standard most often used to measure website accessibility. Accessibility is ongoing work: as the site changes, we review it and correct the problems we find.

What this site does

  • Images carry text descriptions for people using screen readers.
  • Every form field has a clear label, and the site is designed to work with common assistive technologies, including keyboard navigation and screen readers.
  • Text can be enlarged with your browser's zoom, and no account, download, or special software is needed to use the site.
  • The site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or cross-site tracking.

Everything here can be done by phone

Nothing on this website is online-only. To book an appointment, ask about a service, or have anything on this site explained, call the salon at 305-663-7171 during opening hours — Tuesday to Wednesday and Saturday, 9 am to 7 pm; Thursday to Friday, 9 am to 8 pm; Sunday and Monday by appointment.

Tell us about a problem

If any part of this site is difficult to use with your technology, please call us at 305-663-7171 and tell us which page and what went wrong. We will do our best to fix it, or to provide the same information or service in another way that works for you.

Other services

This website links to services run by other companies — including Instagram, Facebook, and Google Maps. Their accessibility is described in their own accessibility statements.
