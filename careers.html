Join the team.

This is an opportunity to join the team at Armin Salon & Rejuvenation — and to bring your clientele with you, to a house in the middle of one of the most remarkable transformations in Miami.

Stand on our corner and look around. The old Sunset Place mall is coming down, and in its place will rise seven residential towers, more than fifteen hundred new homes, a hotel, and a theater — the largest development in South Miami’s history, designed by the studio behind New York’s High Line. A block from our door, a new residential building has just opened. Month by month, this neighborhood gains residents, foot traffic, and a nightlife that keeps its streets full long after dark.

For the right stylist, that is not scenery. It is a book waiting to grow — thousands of new neighbors looking for their salon, in an area whose clientele has always been discerning, and loyal.

Inside, you will find what the address promises: a house run like a family, clients who have stayed for decades, the latest treatment technology, and a standard of craft that has made Armin the name this street trusts.

If you have a clientele of your own, and you are ready to give them a finer room — and yourself a finer address — we should talk. Speak with Lazaro, our Operations Director.
