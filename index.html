Cut with intention.

Every appointment begins with a consultation and an honest look at the hair’s condition. From there: a cut that grows out gracefully, a blowout that holds its line, and styling that never looks overworked.

Services — Colour

Colour, calculated.

Correct colour is not chosen from a swatch — it is formulated. Your colourist reads the hair’s history first: what is on it, what it has carried, how it will lift. Only then is the formula built, strand by strand, tone by tone.

That precision is the difference between colour that merely covers and colour that belongs to you — dimension where the light should live, brightness where it flatters, and a refresh schedule that keeps it honest between visits. Balayage and blonde, composed for Miami light.

Services — Skin & Spa

Skin, treated seriously.

Skin is assessed before it is touched. Our aestheticians work with advanced treatment technology and one simple discipline: nothing aggressive for its own sake, and nothing promised that the skin cannot deliver.

Facials, massage and waxing are performed in the quiet rooms of our South Miami day spa — unhurried, exact, and finished with a plan for your skin at home.
