Services — Colour

Colour, calculated.

Correct colour is not chosen from a swatch — it is formulated. Your colourist reads the hair’s history first: what is on it, what it has carried, how it will lift. Only then is the formula built, strand by strand, tone by tone.

That precision is the difference between colour that merely covers and colour that belongs to you — dimension where the light should live, brightness where it flatters, and a refresh schedule that keeps it honest between visits.
