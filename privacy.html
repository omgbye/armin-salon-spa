Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Armin Salon & Rejuvenation ("the salon," "we") respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what happens with information when you use this website or visit the salon.

This website collects nothing itself

This is a brochure website. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no cross-site tracking. It sets no cookies of its own. Nothing you type into this website is sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Appointment requests

When you ask for an appointment — through this website, by phone, or through our booking provider — the details you give (such as your name, phone number, the service you would like, and your preferred time) are used only to schedule, confirm, and manage your appointment, and to contact you about it. An appointment is confirmed only when the salon confirms it with you by phone.

The email list

If you subscribe to our email list, your email address is used only to send the salon's occasional news and offers. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.

Other services this site links to

This website links to services run by other companies — including Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps, and our online booking provider. When you use those services, their own privacy policies govern the information they collect. Our website host may also keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and browser types) as part of securely operating the site.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time what contact information we hold for you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Call the salon at 305-663-7171.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
